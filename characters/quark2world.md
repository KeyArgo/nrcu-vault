## Friction / stakes (story engine)
quark2world is the ONE who has to run this stuff in a real enterprise — where "internal security people" and angry customers are the monsters, not dragons. He carries the whole stack on his back: self-hosted, hardened, always one model-swap from a fix. His want: a Hermes so trusted it passes enterprise review and makes the customers "finally shut up about slow inference." His friction: the tools are made by nerds, but he has to make them work for suits.
