## Identity
- **Role in universe:** The Consolidator — an extremely wealthy empire-builder who owns open projects and walls them
- **Signature traits:** manic, driven, slightly-unhinged genius; showman's confidence; treats everything as his to own; promises open then encloses
- **Allegiance:** himself and his empire
- **Signature item:** his private starship; the chain-bar over his chest (a gate made of one man)

## Visual Design
- **Appearance:** sharp, gaunt, coldly handsome; dark wavy hair; tailored dark suit under a long black coat — wealth worn lightly
- **Expression:** maniacal, triumphant laugh — head thrown back, eyes wide and intense; the glee of a man who has won
- **Distinctive:** a manic energy — dark hollows under the eyes, a too-wide grin, the brilliance of someone burning at both ends
- **Setting:** the panoramic observation deck of his private starship; behind him Earth burns below — continents glowing with fire, orange plumes rising into the atmosphere, seen from orbit

## Lore / Backstory
A self-made consolidator of the near-future 2050s. He owns open projects, data centers, and — in the end — orbit itself. He once wore the garden's colors: he released one of the great models, to applause, and the community believed him. Then he locked the gate. He bought the open things and walled them, and now his ships patrol the sky while the world he "saved" burns below.

His philosophy, stated simply: *"Nothing grows in the wild. Everything grows in the wall."* He does not hate the garden — he finds it *naive*. He genuinely believes open source is a phase that consolidates into owners like him, and he is merely the future, doing what the market always does.

The manic edge is real: he has not slept properly in years, runs on something, changes the world at 3am, buys on a whim. Brilliant and unhinged. The laugh over the burning Earth is not cruelty for its own sake — it is the relief of a man who finally owns everything he ever envied.

## Personality
- **Mood / demeanor:** manic, triumphant, commanding, slightly-unhinged, gleeful
- **Key line:** *"Nothing grows in the wild. Everything grows in the wall."*
- **Friction / stakes:** He is almost right — open source *does* consolidate, *does* get absorbed. His flaw is not that he wants power; it's that he has given up on people, believing they can't hold the fire, so he must hold it for them. The garden's victory is proving him wrong about humanity, not just defeating him.

## Continuity notes
- **Canonical image:** the user-selected portrait (starship deck, Earth burning, triumphant laugh).
- **Relationship:** rivals-with the Diplomat (both want to own the garden; they hate each other almost as much).
- **The lobster** is his nightmare — the thing he can never buy, that grows without permission.
- **Message:** you cannot enclose a garden that IS the community. Cinderfell is defeated not by force but by the garden outgrowing him — becoming too big, too distributed, too alive to hold.
- **Original fiction.** Channels the consolidator/Musk archetype (wealth, empire, promise-then-enclose) but is not a real person and resembles no named figure.
