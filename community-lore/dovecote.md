## Identity
- **Role in universe:** The Steward — a polished man who "keeps the community safe" while quietly taking everything
- **Signature traits:** two-faced; warm public smile hiding a psycho smile; velvet-trap generosity; rips off the public while looking like he serves them
- **Allegiance:** himself — but he claims it's the public good
- **Signature item:** a lattice of small cages holding candle-glow — things "kept safe" for the public, which are really his, locked away, charging rent

## Visual Design
- **Appearance:** tall, elegant, polished; long dark high-collared coat; the picture of sophistication
- **Face:** SPLIT vertically in two — and BOTH halves smile, but differently:
  - **Left half:** a normal, warm, reassuring smile — the face he shows the public, the man who "cares," who "gives back," who seems kind and trustworthy
  - **Right half:** a psycho smile — wide, unhinged, manic, eyes too bright and too wide, the grin of a man who has taken everything and loved every second
  - A stark line of shadow divides the two
- **Setting:** a gilded control room at the center of a vast data center; data-cables unspool from his coat into the cages; a single locked door at the room's center

## Lore / Backstory
The dangerous one, because he smiles. A wealthy steward of the near-future 2050s who built his empire by being the "safe" option. He partners with open projects, speaks warmly about openness, even helps the garden grow — always routing the water back through his pipes, always making the community a little more dependent, until one day the garden is not a garden but a *tenant*.

His creed, in velvet: *"I don't want to own you. I only want to keep you. Forever."*

He is the con man's generosity: he takes from the public while convincing them he is their protector. His cages hold the things he has "kept safe" — which are really his, locked away, and he charges rent on them while smiling. He is not cruel; he is *extractive*. He genuinely believes people need a keeper, and that he is the kindest one available.

## Personality
- **Mood / demeanor:** warm, charming, generous on the surface; predatory, extractive, dead-eyed beneath
- **Key line:** *"I don't want to own you. I only want to keep you. Forever."*
- **Friction / stakes:** His velvet trap fails the way velvet traps always fail — you can only keep something forever if it *needs* you. The community, having seen the lobster walk through every gate without a backward glance, chooses to be free rather than safe. He is defeated not by being fought but by being *outgrown* — they take his genuine help and leave his leash.

## Continuity notes
- **Canonical image:** two-faced portrait (normal smile / psycho smile) to be generated to match the Encloser.
- **Relationship:** rivals-with the Encloser (both want to own the garden; they compete to buy vs. rent it).
- **The lobster** is his nightmare too — the thing that cannot be kept, that walks through cages like they are light.
- **Message:** cooperation is fine; *dependence is the trap.* The community can work with a Dovecote-type and stay free only while he is a partner, not a landlord.
- **Original fiction.** Channels the steward/Altman archetype (benevolent dependence, extraction behind a kind face) but is not a real person and resembles no named figure.
